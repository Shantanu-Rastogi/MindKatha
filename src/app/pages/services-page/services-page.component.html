Gold-Standard Testing Protocol

The Formal Diagnostic Assessment Dossier.

How our standardized adult ADHD evaluations, MCMI-IV personality testing, and cognitive batteries are conducted from initial interview to official RCI psychologist certification.

01

Clinical Intake & History

60-minute developmental clinical interview mapping symptom trajectories, childhood milestones, and differential diagnosis factors.

02

Testing Battery Execution

Supervised administration of standardized instruments (DIVA-5, ASRS v1.1, MCMI-IV, MMPI-2, and projective indices).

03

Certified Report Synthesis

Psychometric scoring, quantitative clinical profile creation, and RCI-certified diagnostic report compilation in 3–5 business days.

04

1-on-1 Clinical Debrief

In-depth feedback consultation providing workplace accommodations, educational adjustments, and tailored therapeutic next steps.

No Need for Prior Preparation

100% Confidential Clinical Space
