Operational Transparency

Logistics & Care FAQs.

Do you accept insurance or corporate panels?

MindKatha operates exclusively as an out-of-network clinical provider. We intentionally do not join insurance panels in order to prevent corporate adjusters from dictating your diagnosis, frequency, or modality of therapy. You are billed directly at the end of each session. On request, we generate an itemized clinical receipt that you can submit to your private insurer or corporate reimbursement program.

What happens in my first intake session?

The initial consultation is a structured 50-minute evaluation focused on mapping your personal narrative. We spend the first half exploring relational dynamics and life context, and the second half assessing current nervous system regulation and burnout triggers. Together, we co-create an actionable, pressure-free care roadmap.

Is digital video therapy as effective as in-person?

Yes. Extensive clinical outcome studies indicate that secure video telehealth maintains identical therapeutic efficacy rates to physical studio visits for CBT, ACT, and Narrative modalities. The physical studio in Viman Nagar, Pune is available whenever you prefer an acoustic, in-person grounding environment.

How often will we meet, and for how long?

Standard psychotherapy sessions are scheduled once per week or bi-weekly for 50 minutes to maintain narrative continuity and active behavioral rewiring. As symptom relief consolidates, session frequency naturally tapers to monthly maintenance check-ins.
