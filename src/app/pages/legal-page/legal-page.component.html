Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)

1. Commitment to Data Privacy

At MindKatha, client confidentiality and personal data security are fundamental ethical principles. We collect and process health-related and personal data solely to provide professional psychological counseling and psychotherapy services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and location for appointment scheduling.
  • Clinical Intake Notes: Background details voluntarily provided during consultation inquiries and intake forms.
  • Session Records: De-identified clinical notes stored securely and separately from personally identifiable details.

3. Data Security & Telehealth

All digital intake forms, communication channels, and video telehealth platforms utilize encrypted transmission (AES-256 / TLS). We do not record video therapy sessions. No personal or clinical data is ever sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you retain the right to access your registered contact data, request corrections, or withdraw consent for non-clinical data processing at any time by contacting our practice at psychotherapy.leona@gmail.com.

Clinical Informed Consent

Ethical Framework for Psychological Therapy

1. Nature of Psychotherapy

Psychotherapy is a collaborative process between client and therapist. While therapy often results in significant symptom relief, improved relationships, and greater emotional clarity, it also involves discussing difficult life experiences and uncomfortable emotions. Outcomes depend on mutual commitment and collaborative effort.

2. Scope of Outpatient Care

MindKatha provides outpatient psychotherapy by RCI-registered practitioners. We do not provide medical prescriptions or emergency crisis intervention. Clients requiring pharmacological management or emergency psychiatric stabilization are referred to certified psychiatric facilities.

3. Limits of Confidentiality

Information shared in therapy is strictly confidential. In accordance with clinical ethics and Indian law, confidentiality may only be broken under the following critical circumstances:

  • Clear and imminent danger of harm to self (suicidality) or others.
  • Suspected abuse, neglect, or harm involving a minor, elderly individual, or dependent adult.
  • Direct court subpoena or legal mandate requiring disclosure.

4. Telehealth Requirements

For online sessions, clients agree to attend from a quiet, private space with a stable internet connection to safeguard their own confidentiality and emotional focus.

Terms of Service & Practice Policies

Operational Logistics & Appointment Scheduling

1. Appointment Scheduling & Punctuality

Standard psychotherapy sessions are 60 minutes in length (75 minutes for couples therapy). Sessions commence and conclude at the scheduled time to respect subsequent appointments.

2. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

We request a minimum of 24 to 48 hours notice for cancelling or rescheduling an appointment. Because dedicated clinical time is reserved for you, cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or unattended sessions may be subject to the full session fee.

3. Payment & Reimbursement

Fees are payable at the time of booking or at the conclusion of each session via approved payment methods (UPI, NetBanking, Cards). We operate as a direct self-pay practice and provide itemized clinical superbills on request for clients submitting claims to private insurers.

4. Emergency Crisis Protocol

MindKatha is not a 24/7 crisis center. If you or someone you know is in immediate life-threatening danger, please contact national emergency helplines immediately (Tele-MANAS: 14416, Vandrevala Foundation: +91 9999 666 555, or AASRA: +91 98204 66726).
